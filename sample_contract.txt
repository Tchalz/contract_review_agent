SERVICE AGREEMENT

This Service Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by and between
Acme Consulting LLC ("Provider") and Northwind Retail Inc. ("Client").

1. SCOPE OF SERVICES
Provider shall deliver software consulting and development services as
described in Exhibit A, which may be modified by Provider at any time
without prior notice to Client.

2. PAYMENT TERMS
Client shall pay Provider within 90 days of invoice receipt. Late
payments shall not accrue interest or penalties under any circumstances.

3. TERMINATION
This Agreement may be terminated by Provider at any time, for any
reason, with immediate effect and without notice. Client may not
terminate this Agreement prior to the end of the initial 24-month term
under any circumstances.

4. LIABILITY
Provider's total liability under this Agreement, whether in contract,
tort, or otherwise, shall be unlimited and shall include indirect,
incidental, special, and consequential damages of any kind.

5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
All work product, inventions, and materials created by Provider under
this Agreement shall remain the sole and exclusive property of Provider,
including any materials developed using Client's proprietary data.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY
Each party agrees to keep confidential information disclosed by the
other party confidential for a period of 12 months following disclosure.

7. NON-COMPETE
Client agrees not to engage any competing consulting firm for a period
of 5 years following termination of this Agreement, worldwide, in any
industry Provider currently or may in the future operate in.

8. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the state in which
Provider's principal office is located, without regard to conflict of
law principles.

9. AUTOMATIC RENEWAL
This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive 24-month terms
unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least
180 days prior to the end of the then-current term.

10. INDEMNIFICATION
Client shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Provider from any and
all claims, damages, losses, and expenses, including attorneys' fees,
arising out of or related to this Agreement, regardless of fault.

date first written above.
